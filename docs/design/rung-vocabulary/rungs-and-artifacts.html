Every gate, what it checks, and where the rename bites

The ladder is cumulative — a gate checks everything the gates above it checked, plus one new thing. No check is ever dropped. Seven gates, not eight: loop is a container holding build, verify and adjust, and the machine checks only at its two edges. Risk is checked twice at two depths, and scope is where the work is locked in. The three renamed words cost wildly different amounts.
