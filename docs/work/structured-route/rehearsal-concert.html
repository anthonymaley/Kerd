Rehearsal, the score, the concert

Rehearsal is turn by turn: the idea, whether it can work, goals and constraints, the design. When you are ready, the composer turns it into a score, and the concert performs it: agents loop to the score while you watch where they are, and you come back to a result. It should feel like playing music, not operating a machine.
