restored records. It does not load Conductor or its journey guide for composition.
Approval carries that context into Conductor once. A direct workflow request can
enter Understand/Shape with exclusions and no operational approval; it does not

reason to substitute related work or ask again to open the workflow.

before substantial investigation or edits. Multi-step design and diagnosis need
a useful work view and an inline/delegation decision too, not just implementation.
