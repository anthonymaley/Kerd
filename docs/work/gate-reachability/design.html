Conductor's step check, in your project

Conductor checks where a piece of work stands at two moments: when it picks the work up, and before it starts a build. If groundwork is missing it says what, in plain words, and offers to do it. If you go ahead anyway, that is written into the work record so the next session sees it. Agreed 2026-09-18 13:39.

When it checks

  • Picking up a piece of work that has a work-item record in your project.
  • Before starting a build on it.
  • Not for small fixes, or work with no record: no check, no nagging.

What you see

  • One plain line: the step the work is on, and what the next one needs.
  • An offer to do the missing groundwork now.
  • If your project can't be identified for sure, it says so and doesn't guess.

Go ahead anyway

  • Your call, never refused.
  • Written into the work record: date, the step skipped, your words.
  • The next session's pickup shows it.
