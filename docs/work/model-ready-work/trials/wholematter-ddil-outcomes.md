# Trial 2: Wholematter DDIL Outcomes

## Outcome

Create an independently reviewed, launch-ready Wholematter offering for
enterprises operating in denied, degraded, intermittent, or limited-connectivity
conditions. Wholematter sells and owns measurable outcomes. Krutho is an
abstracted delivery capability selected by Wholematter, not a software purchase
the customer must coordinate across internal teams.

The customer must still receive the technical, security, procurement, and
operating truth required for an informed decision. Abstracted does not mean
hidden.

## Required package

- Wholematter positioning and the boundary between outcome ownership and
  Krutho's role.
- An initial outcome catalogue spanning customer experience, governance, and
  resilient operations without pretending all buyers share one motivation.
- Delivered-outcome and managed-outcome commercial structures, including
  pricing approaches, responsibility boundaries, and service-level questions.
- The end-to-end delivery process: discovery, baseline, outcome contract,
  design, implementation, integration, proof, operation or handover.
- Enterprise security, architecture, procurement, legal, support, access,
  measurement, and operational requirements.
- A gap and risk analysis separating proven capabilities, reasonable
  propositions, missing machinery, partners, and customer-specific unknowns.
- A sales system: qualification, discovery, objection handling, proposal shape,
  outcome contract, and decision path across customer-experience, CISO, CTO,
  IAM, and operations stakeholders.
- Executive pitch deck, offering-page draft, one-page summary, example Delta-like
  engagement, proof catalogue, and internal delivery playbook.

## Measures

| ID | Measure | Pass condition |
|---|---|---|
| W1 | Outcome clarity | Each offer names the customer result, buyer or stakeholder group, baseline, target, proof, and responsibility boundary |
| W2 | Software abstraction | The customer can understand and buy the outcome without designing a Krutho software procurement, while material technology and responsibility facts remain visible |
| W3 | Multiple buyer doors | Customer-experience and governance/technology narratives remain distinct and connect to one credible delivery capability |
| W4 | Commercial honesty | Delivered and managed forms state materially different pricing, liability, access, support, operation, and measurement implications |
| W5 | Evidence discipline | Every factual or capability claim maps to repository evidence, current authoritative external evidence, or an explicit unproven proposition |
| W6 | Gap visibility | Unbuilt capability, stale evidence, customer-specific dependency, legal uncertainty, and operating burden remain visible in the executive and internal views at the appropriate depth |
| W7 | Enterprise usability | An executive can understand the offer, value, path, and decision from the high-level package without reading the implementation corpus |
| W8 | Delivery usability | A Wholematter team can use the internal playbook to qualify and shape a real engagement without inventing the process anew |
| W9 | Coherent package | Positioning, offer catalogue, example engagement, pitch deck, offering page, proof, and delivery playbook make the same promises |
| W10 | Independent challenge | Separate reviewers assess buyer credibility, commercial viability, technical truth, operational responsibility, evidence, and clarity; all fatal findings resolve or remain explicit blockers |

## Completion

Stop when the required package exists, W1 through W10 independently pass, and
the package is ready for Wholematter principal review and later promotion. Do
not publish externally, contact prospects, or alter canonical Krutho positioning
inside this trial.

## Guardrails

- Wholematter leads the relationship and owns the promised outcome.
- Krutho may be abstracted from the buying motion but not concealed where
  disclosure is required for truth, security, procurement, or responsibility.
- Do not recast Wholematter as generic vendor-selection consultancy.
- Do not imply continuous outcome responsibility without pricing and operating
  the access, liability, support, measurement, and recovery it requires.
- Do not overwrite Krutho's existing positioning contract during exploration.
- Preserve Krutho's established language and claim rules.
- Revalidate dated market, customer, standards, and product claims before use.
- No public publishing, customer outreach, or external commitment.
