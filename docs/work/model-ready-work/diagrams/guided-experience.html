Accessible description: The guided interview reflects the person's need, retrieves existing answers, asks one small group of questions that can change the work, and stops when ready. Cross-model work uses separate bounded jobs sharing one fixed brief; Conductor combines evidence. Four visual depths let the person move from product outcome through flow and architecture to detailed sequences and proof. The process retains rigor underneath while the approval view stays short, and hidden detail may not conceal a consequential loss.
