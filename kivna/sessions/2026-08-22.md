  `main` clones into an unborn `master` with **no upstream**, so `@{u}` fails
  silently and any behind-remote check reads as "not behind". This was
  misdiagnosed in the Backlog for weeks as a sandbox problem with `git fetch
  --dry-run`, which emits its `->` line perfectly well.

## Insights

- **A box that only holds text is a slide, not a diagram.** Two drawings, the
  same toolkit, opposite verdicts. The one that worked used a type and let
  containment carry the argument; the one that failed ignored all 39 types and
  hand-rolled panels of prose. This is the standing *"what fails the gate is
  prose"* decision one level down — **prose does not stop being prose because a
  rectangle is drawn around it.**
- **Neither linter can catch that, and the render check cannot either.** The
  failed diagram passed the source linter and rendered perfectly. There is no
  machine check for *does this box mean anything*; the only reviewer is the
  producer. Same declared limit as reachability.
- **The diagram premise finally has a number.** 39 requirements as text took a
  week; one lifecycle — five states, seven transitions, an escalation rule — took
  one exchange. *"see how easy that was for me to agree."* The drawing is not the
  simpler artifact; in prose that lifecycle is three paragraphs the reader must
  hold in their head. Drawn, the structure **is** the argument.
- **Dismissing by domain rather than by shape cost five wrong judgements.** Seven
  types were blacklisted in one line as "data-platform specific". Read properly,
  five had real uses — a permissions grid is a permissions grid whether the rows
  are platform components or funnel rungs. Same error as the 2026-08-08
  convergence finding: reading the label is not reading the thing.
- **Two standards were adopted where invention was about to happen.** The
  technique for universal force without a totality word was declared *"nowhere
  taught"* and is taught twice over; the concern/viewpoint/view vocabulary and
  its completeness rule were about to be invented and are ISO 42010 verbatim.
  **Both were caught by Law 4, and both times the assumption had already been
  written down as fact.**
- **He flagged rather than skipped.** Given 39 requirements and no obligation, the
  six he could not write a reason for came back as questions, not blanks. A blank
  would have been a gap; a flag is a question — and four of the six were resolved
  from the record in one pass.
