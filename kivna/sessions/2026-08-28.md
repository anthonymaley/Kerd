- **A never-launched Chrome hangs every headless call, `--version` included.**
  Gatekeeper's first-launch prompt is a GUI dialog; headless cannot answer it,
  so the process sits forever with no error. `xattr -l "/Applications/Google
  Chrome.app" | grep -c quarantine` → 0 is the check. Launch once by hand.
- **Retrying a hung render is not diagnosis.** The conductor's second attempt
  (a throwaway `--user-data-dir`) was a guess dressed as a fix; the cause was
  found only when `--version` was tried and also hung. Ask why before the second try.
- **A player may stage files it did not touch.** One haiku player left
  `TODO.md` *staged*; harmless here, caught at Step 6's `git status`. Check
  the index, not just the tree, after a player returns.
- **`open` on the Studio is a shim to the laptop** — it copies the file across
  and opens it there. "It should be in Preview behind other windows" was wrong
  on this host; nothing on its display is visible to the user.
