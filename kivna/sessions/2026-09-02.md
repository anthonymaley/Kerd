## Key Decisions

Full rationale in CONTEXT.md. **A rung leaves one of four things behind** — GO,
waiver, legacy closure, or nothing and stays blocked — and conflating them is the
failure the records exist to prevent · **the two exception types keep separate
homes**, because *"a waiver proves an obligation existed and was consciously
skipped; a legacy closure proves the obligation did not yet exist and must not be
reconstructed"* · **authorship is never the test, the producer's key is** ·
**a legacy closure closes the routing gap and never the evidence gap**, and must
never say nobody could have produced a design — they could have; the process did
not require or retain it · **do not distort a conceptual route around today's
file-glob behaviour**.

## Gotchas

- **A waiver or legacy-closure record has no legal filename under
  `docs/gates/`.** `AU3` requires every filename directly there to match
  `^\d{4}-\d{2}-\d{2}-<slug>-<rung>\.md$`, and the only legal suffixes are rung
  names — so landing either type there turns CI red. Both went into
  subdirectories (`waivers/`, `closures/`), which AU3's non-recursive glob
  correctly does not count as gate records. **The sibling of CONTEXT.md's
  existing gap:** gate records can only say GO; a refused gate has no dated home,
  and now neither does a waived one. Mirrored to `docs/playbook.md`.
- **Declaring a `concerns:` block demotes the item until the view is sealed.**
  Adding a concern with no approval line fails the design rung immediately, so
  the item drops a rung mid-sitting. That is the gate working — but the board
  must not be refreshed during the dip. Second trigger for the same rule the seal
  downgrade already carries. Mirrored.
- **A scripted edit that appends a `Clock:` line can leave the drafting stamp
  beside the key's.** Two `Clock:` lines landed in the waiver — 13:04 from the
  draft and 15:01 from the key — and only a read of the file's tail caught it.
  **When a record is drafted then keyed, the draft's timestamps are content to
  remove, not scaffolding that disappears.** Mirrored.

## Insights

  producer"* was refuted by pointing out that a model typed every design document

  The sitting began with an instruction to prepare five design packages; two of
  the five could only have been closed by backfilling GO records — which is the
  exact move that motivated `inline-composer`'s frame in August. Surfacing that
  conflict rather than executing it produced the waiver and the legacy closure.
  **The instruction was right and its sequence was wrong, and saying so early
  cost one exchange.**

  of the five could not be keyed. Manufacturing packages for them would have
  produced five approvals and two lies. The producer's own success condition —
  *"five independently reviewable design packages, not five automatic
  approvals"* — is what made reporting the blockage the correct output.
- **A gate that can refuse is worth more than a gate that passes.** Declaring the
  concerns block made `inline-composer`'s design gate demote its own item. Before
  that, the gate verified a file existed. Four of 22 items declared concerns at
  the start of the day; five do now, and the other seventeen still have a design
  gate that can be satisfied by touching a file.
