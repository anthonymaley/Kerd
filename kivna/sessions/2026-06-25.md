## Insights

- **Voice extraction's core problem is signal vs noise.** A draft contains genuine style and accidental error, and on the page they look the same — a comma-splice can be a deliberate accumulation rhythm or a typo. Codify the noise and the profile makes the model write worse, in the user's name. The whole value is in the separation.
- **Anti-AI and sound-like-the-user mostly point the same way.** Named specifics, repetition instead of elegant variation, abrupt section pivots — these are simultaneously the user's voice and the things readers and detectors clock as human. So "anti-AI first" preserves most of the voice rather than fighting it. The one genuine collision (vague self-promotional superlatives) resolves by converting them to the concrete fact that earned them, which is the user's strongest trait anyway. One rule, conflict gone, voice stronger.
