### TWI Textbooks (Japanese originals)
Toyota's supervisor training is built on TWI (Training Within Industry), introduced to Japan in 1950. The original Japanese textbooks are still actively used and sold. Not available in bookstores; direct purchase only.

- **TWI-JI:** 仕事の教え方 手引書 (Job Instruction guidebook)
- **TWI-JM:** 改善の仕方 手引書 (Job Methods guidebook), ~3,850 yen
- **TWI-JR:** 人の扱い方 (Job Relations)
- **TWI-JI Application Guide:** 活用の手引, 495 yen
- **Seller:** 日本産業訓練協会 (Japan Industrial Training Association)
- **Buy:** [sankun.jp/collections/twi-text](https://sankun.jp/collections/twi-text)
- **Status:** NEED. These are the exact materials Toyota uses for supervisor training.

## Priority 4: Internal/Restricted (reference only)

These are not purchasable but are documented here for completeness.

### Toyota Way 2001 (the "Green Book")
- **Title:** トヨタウェイ2001
- **Type:** Internal Toyota philosophy document, 13 pages
- **Year:** 2001
- **Status:** Japanese original is proprietary. English version publicly available at [University of Michigan](http://www-personal.umich.edu/~mrother/KATA_Files/The_Toyota_Way_2001.pdf)
- **Known nuance:** "知恵と改善" (Chie to Kaizen, "Wisdom and Continuous Improvement") rendered as just "Continuous Improvement" in English. The concept of 知恵 (wisdom/ingenuity from experience) is dropped entirely.

### TBP Training Materials
- **Title:** Toyota Business Practices (トヨタ・ビジネス・プラクティス)
- **Type:** Toyota Institute internal training
- **Year:** ~2005
- **Status:** Not publicly sold. Leaked English presentation slides exist on [SlideShare](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/toyota-business-practice-englishppt/255240292) and [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/presentation/280394213/The-Steps-of-Toyota-Business-Practices)
- **The 8 steps in Japanese:**
  1. 問題を明確にする (Clarify the problem)
  2. 問題をブレイクダウンする (Break down the problem)
  3. 達成目標を決める (Set the target)
  4. 真因を考え抜く (Investigate root causes thoroughly)
  5. 対策を立てる (Develop countermeasures)
  6. 対策をやりぬく (Implement countermeasures)
  7. 結果とプロセスを評価する (Evaluate results and processes)
  8. 成果を定着させる (Standardize successful results)
