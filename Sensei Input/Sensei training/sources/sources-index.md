# TPS Sources Index

Canonical sources ingested for the TPS knowledge base. Each source file is a standalone extraction organized by the original book's structure. The knowledge base (`docs/tps-knowledge-base.md`) is a curated synthesis drawn from all sources, organized by concept.

## Sources

### 1. "The Toyota Way" by Jeffrey Liker (2nd Edition)
**File:** `liker-toyota-way.md`
**Type:** Western academic analysis of Toyota's management system
**Extracted:** 2026-04-13
**Lines:** ~1,363

The most complete external analysis of TPS. Organizes Toyota's approach into the 4P model (Philosophy, Process, People, Problem Solving) and 14 Principles. Strong on the management and leadership side. Covers hoshin kanri, A3 thinking, PDCA, the coaching kata, and how Toyota develops people. Written from decades of academic study and plant visits.

**Unique contributions to the knowledge base:**
- 4P model and 14 Principles framework
- TPS House visual model with pillars and foundation
- Toyota Way 2001 internal philosophy document (5 values)
- A3 thinking and problem-solving methodology
- Coaching kata and mentor/learner relationship
- Hoshin kanri (policy deployment)
- Toyota's approach to developing leaders and people
- PDCA as the scientific thinking method at the center

### 2. 1973 Toyota Production System Handbook
**File:** `1973-tps-handbook.md`
**Type:** Toyota internal training manual for supervisors
**Authors:** OMCD staff (Operations Management Consulting Division), preface by Taiichi Ohno
**Translated/Edited:** Mark Warren (2019, Revision 4)
**Extracted:** 2026-04-14
**Lines:** ~600

The oldest and most authoritative source. Written by Toyota's own OMCD for internal use before any book existed on TPS. Predates Ohno's own book by 5 years. The language is direct and instructional, aimed at supervisors learning the system. Contains the original thinking before it was filtered through Western interpretation.

**Unique contributions to the knowledge base:**
- Original 4 types of unnecessary activity (before 7 wastes)
- "Labor saving" vs "People saving" distinction with visual
- Work redistribution: why you must NOT spread surplus evenly (the correct vs bad example with charts)
- 4-S (original, before 5S/6S) with the note that later consultants complicated it
- Supervisor's improvement circle diagram
- "Reduce the best personnel first" when reducing headcount
- Assembly line improvement case study (Sept-Dec 1946, complaints of "labor intensification" disappearing)
- Standard work combination chart methodology (11-step detailed procedure)
- Poka-yoke 6 categories (A-F)
- Inspector as teacher, not pass/fail judge
- Conveyor safety example showing how blame goes to person when real cause is incomplete automation
- "A line which does not stop is either perfectly good or extremely bad"
- "Logic escape" concept from Ohno's preface
- Kanban as "jewel of wisdom"
- The 3M's: Muda (waste), Mura (unevenness), Muri (overburden)

### 3. "Toyota Production System: Beyond Large-Scale Production" by Taiichi Ohno
**File:** `ohno-tps-beyond-large-scale.md`
**Type:** First-person account by TPS creator
**Published:** 1978 (Japanese), 1988 (English)
**Extracted:** 2026-04-14
**Lines:** ~600

Ohno's own book. The only first-person account of building TPS from the inside over 30+ years. Rich with the history of how each idea emerged from necessity, the resistance it met, and how Ohno expanded it through his growing authority. Contains the genealogy connecting Toyoda Sakichi, Toyoda Kiichiro, and Ohno himself. Unique for its Ford analysis and the argument that Ford's successors misunderstood his true intention.

**Unique contributions to the knowledge base:**
- Ohno's first-person narrative of building TPS (1943-1978)
- Supermarket analogy origin story (1953, from American supermarkets)
- 10-year timeline of kanban adoption (1953-1962)
- Autonomic nervous system analogy for business organization
- Plans as spine: "The sturdier the spine, the more easily it bends"
- Fine adjustment through kanban (automatic response to demand shifts)
- Ford analysis: "Ford's successors misinterpreted the work flow system" by stopping flow with large lot sizes
- Ford quotes from "Today and Tomorrow" (1926) on waste, standards, efficiency
- Tortoise and Hare: "the slower but consistent tortoise causes less waste"
- Management by Ninjutsu: "management should be done not by arithmetic but by ninjutsu"
- Corolla story: 80 workers → 100 workers making 10,000+ engines (not 160)
- "Apparent efficiency" vs true efficiency when demand is flat
- Take Good Care of Old Equipment: "A machine's value is determined by the earning power it still retains"
- Chapter 4 genealogy: Toyoda Sakichi → Toyoda Kiichiro → Ohno, tracing the search for Japanese-origin production technology
- "Few people in the world can raise productivity when production quantities decrease"
- Full 24-term glossary as Ohno defined them

## How Sources Relate

The three sources cover the same system from three angles:

| Aspect | 1973 Handbook | Ohno's Book | Liker |
|--------|--------------|-------------|-------|
| Voice | Toyota training supervisors | Ohno reflecting on his career | Academic analyzing from outside |
| Audience | Shop floor leaders | Business managers worldwide | Western managers and students |
| Emphasis | How to do it (procedures, charts, rules) | Why it exists (history, philosophy, necessity) | What it means (principles, frameworks, patterns) |
| Time frame | Snapshot of TPS as practiced in 1973 | 30-year narrative (1943-1978) | Multi-decade study through 2020s |
| On problem solving | Scientific attitude, 5-Whys, make problems visible | Root cause, plant-first principle | A3 thinking, PDCA, coaching kata |
| On waste | 4 categories, detailed examples from factory | 7 wastes, capacity equation, re-examination | 8 wastes (adds unused creativity), process mapping |
| On standards | Standard work charts, combination charts, work instructions | Standard work sheet, "standards are something to set up yourself" | Standardized work as foundation, visual management |
| On people | Respect for people as TPS foundation, human relations chapter | Teamwork, baton passing, multi-skilled workers | People development, coaching, challenge |

The 1973 Handbook and Ohno's book share the same worldview but serve different purposes. The handbook is the training manual (how), the book is the memoir (why). Liker provides the outside framework that helps Westerners organize what Toyota practitioners already knew intuitively.

### 4. Tim Platt — TMMNA Pocketmod
**File:** `platt-tmmna-pocketmod.md`
**Type:** Toyota internal operational training material (pocket reference)
**Author:** Tim Platt, TMMNA Information Systems
**Created:** 1998-2001
**Extracted:** 2026-04-15
**Pages:** 8

Toyota training material documented and compiled by Platt as a pocket-sized reference he kept on his person and gave to a select few. Platt came from TMMNA to Toyota Europe for several years; this was part of his knowledge transfer. The content is Toyota's — Platt recorded it, not created it. Same attribution principle as Liker.

**Unique contributions to the knowledge base:**
- 7 A3 Story Types (Information: Educate to Details, Illumination of Unknown, Compare & Contrast, Education/Satisfaction; Recommendation: Standard Toyota Proposal, Correcting Discrepancy from Standard, Develop the Roadmap)
- 5-step A3 story creation process with "Rock Solid" principles (3-minute elevator ride, grandparent test)
- 8 Components of Nemawashi as operational method (Relationships 1-4, Communication 5-8)
- ROOKIES leadership profile (Rational, Open, Optimistic, Knowledgeable, Integrity-bound, Even-handed, Sure)
- Tiered Management Visual Control (Executive periodic → Project Mgr ongoing → Activity Mgr weekly → Performer daily)
- Foundational Expectations for All (5 rules including 80/20 per phase, backup documentation)
- TBP 8 steps with Drive and Dedication principles
- Base Toyota Way as CGRRT (Challenge, Kaizen, Genchi-Genbutsu, Respect, Teamwork)

## Adding New Sources

When ingesting a new book:
1. Read the full source
2. Write an extraction to `docs/sources/[author-short-title].md`
3. Add an entry to this index with unique contributions
4. Rebuild `docs/tps-knowledge-base.md` as curated synthesis from all sources
