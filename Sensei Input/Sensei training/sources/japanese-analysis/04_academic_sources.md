A navigation file. Where to find the Japanese primary and secondary sources, with short notes on what each contains.

### 1.2 Oral history — *highest-value primary source outside the book*

**「トヨタ自動車元副社長 大野耐一氏 口述記錄」**
*University of Tokyo, CIRJE (Center for International Research on the Japanese Economy), Discussion Paper series*
Publicly downloadable PDF at: <https://www.cirje.e.u-tokyo.ac.jp/research/dp/97/j4/dp.pdf>

Ohno in his own words, in an interview format, discussing the origins of TPS. This is uncurated — closer to how he actually spoke on the floor. Contains material not in the 1978 book.

## 3. Japanese academic databases to search

### 3.1 CiNii (国立情報学研究所 / NII)
<https://cir.nii.ac.jp/> — the Japanese equivalent of Google Scholar. Free search, many full-text PDFs.

**Recommended queries (copy-paste):**
- `トヨタ生産方式 大野耐一` — returns the book's cataloguing record plus dozens of citing articles
- `トヨタウェイ 知恵と改善` — Toyota Way research
- `自働化 ジャストインタイム` — TPS conceptual papers
- `現地現物 組織学習` — Genchi Genbutsu in organizational learning
- `改善 ノウハウ 暗黙知` — kaizen and tacit knowledge transfer
- `なぜなぜ分析 問題解決` — why-why / problem solving

Key record: **『大野耐一の記録 ― トヨタ生産方式の創始者』** — CiNii BB07190084 ([link](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB07190084)) — a compiled record of Ohno.

### 3.2 J-STAGE (Japan Science & Technology Agency)
<https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/> — free, peer-reviewed Japanese academic papers.

**Recommended queries:**
- `トヨタ生産方式` (*Toyota seisan hōshiki*) — returns operations research and industrial engineering papers
- `リーン生産方式` — the Japanese-language literature on lean
- `カンバン方式` — kanban research
- `組織能力 トヨタ` — organizational capability research (this is the richest academic vein)

### 3.3 国立国会図書館 (NDL) Digital Collections
<https://dl.ndl.go.jp/>

The National Diet Library's digital archive. Some material is:
- Fully open (pre-1950s, public domain)
- "個人送信" (personal transmission) — accessible free to registered NDL users (including non-Japanese residents via registration)
- 図書館送信 — in-library only at Japanese partner libraries

For Ohno 1978: the physical book is held. Depending on current rights clearance it may be viewable via the 個人送信 tier — log in to NDL and search 『トヨタ生産方式』.

### 3.4 Meijo University 経営学論集
The Meijo University Ronso is a reliable free source of long-form Japanese research on Toyota culture. Kenji Ito's 2012 paper on Toyoda Akio and the Toyota Way is at:
<https://wwwbiz.meijo-u.ac.jp/SEBM/ronso/no13_1/03_ITO.pdf>

### 3.5 Drucker Workshop / ドラッカー学会
<https://drucker-ws.org/> — publishes Japanese-language papers connecting Toyota's approach to Drucker's knowledge-worker theory. The paper 「トヨタウェイと知識労働者としての職場リーダ」 is at <https://drucker-ws.org/D_management/DM_12_02.pdf>.

## 4. High-signal Japanese secondary sources (practitioners, not academics)

These are Japanese-language blogs and teaching sites that consistently quote Ohno and Toyota materials accurately, with attribution. Useful as a sanity check on translations and for finding phrasing variants.

- **カイゼンベース (Kaizen Base)** — <https://kaizen-base.com/> — systematic TPS teaching in Japanese, with diagrams
- **ものづくりドットコム (Monodukuri.com)** — <https://www.monodukuri.com/> — technical manufacturing articles, often by veterans
- **改善ライブラリ** — <https://www.ojt-s.jp/kaizenlibrary/> — a practitioner's knowledge base
- **改善.net** — <https://kaizen1.net/> — concept-level kaizen writing
- **Diamond Online** — <https://diamond.jp/> — Diamond 社's online magazine, publishes regular Ohno/TPS essays
- **President Online** — <https://president.jp/> — features good long-form Ohno pieces, including quoted passages
- **東洋経済 / Toyo Keizai** — <https://toyokeizai.net/> — business magazine with strong TPS coverage
- **Toyota Enterprise Inc. (トヨタエンタプライズ)** — <https://kensyu.toyota-ep.co.jp/> — a Toyota-group training company; their seminar outlines expose the real pedagogical structure
- **カイゼン・ライブラリ（OJTソリューションズ）** — <https://www.ojt-s.jp/kaizenlibrary/> — run by former Toyota instructors

## 5. English-language secondary sources that quote Japanese primary material

Useful for triangulation. These authors worked with Toyota in Japan and had access to materials most outsiders don't:

- **Jeffrey K. Liker** — *The Toyota Way* (2004); *Toyota Way to Lean Leadership* (2011); *Toyota Kata Culture* (with Rother). Liker has quoted portions of the internal Toyota Way 2001 document with permission.
- **John Shook** — *Managing to Learn* (2008), the definitive A3 thinking book; Shook was the first non-Japanese Toyota manager. His book reproduces actual A3 templates used in Japan.
- **Mike Rother** — *Toyota Kata* (2009) and *The Toyota Kata Practice Guide* (2017). Emphasizes the *improvement kata* and *coaching kata* as the daily practice underlying the Toyota Way.
- **Art Smalley** — <https://artoflean.com/> — a former Toyota insider's encyclopedia of TPS, with Japanese terminology preserved.
- **Takeuchi, Osono, Shimizu** — *Extreme Toyota: Radical Contradictions That Drive Success at the World's Best Manufacturer* (Wiley, 2008). The chapter "Making Tacit Knowledge Explicit" is the most detailed public treatment of the Toyota Way 2001's authoring process.
- **Michel Baudin** — <https://michelbaudin.com/> — former Ohno-era visitor, publishes nuanced critique of English lean mistranslations. His "Toyota Way 2001: the Necronomicon of Lean" essay (2013) surveys the document's variants.

## 6. Museums & archives

- **産業技術記念館 / Toyota Commemorative Museum of Industry and Technology** (Nagoya) — <https://www.tcmit.org/> — holds Ohno-era artifacts, loom displays (Sakichi's original 自働織機 is here), and archival materials. On-site Japanese-language panels quote period documents.
- **トヨタ会館 / Toyota Kaikan** (Toyota City) — the corporate museum. Less archival; more contemporary.

## 7. Suggested research sequence

If you want to build a serious Japanese-language understanding of Toyota training material:

1. **Buy the Japanese edition of Ohno 1978** on Kindle. Read alongside your English copy. Keep a notebook of phrases where the Japanese says something the English doesn't.
2. **Read the Ohno oral history** from the UTokyo CIRJE PDF. This is free and exposes his actual voice.
3. **Read 若松義人's books** (several on Ohno). 若松 worked directly under Ohno; his retellings include many quoted Ohno lines.
4. **Search CiNii** for papers citing 『トヨタ生産方式』 and follow the ones with Japanese abstracts that directly quote Ohno.
5. **Work through the Toyota corporate history pages** at toyota.co.jp/jpn/company/history/75years/ — these are the official voice.
6. **If you can travel to Japan**, the 産業技術記念館 in Nagoya is a full day; the Ohno-era artifacts there, with Japanese captions, close a surprising amount of the translation gap.
7. **For training material specifically**, the honest path is a Toyota Enterprise Inc. licensed seminar (in Japanese) or an OJT Solutions engagement. Their public syllabi (see Section 4) reveal the real pedagogy.

## 8. What I could not obtain in this research session

- The **full internal Toyota Way 2001 booklet** in Japanese — this is a Toyota-internal document and is not publicly sold. Excerpts in Liker and Osono/Shimizu/Takeuchi are the best publicly available window.
- **Direct scans of Ohno 1978 pages** — the book is in copyright until ~2060 and I won't help locate pirated scans. The legitimate Japanese e-book editions are linked in file `00_overview_and_copyright.md`.
- **Internal A3 training binders / TBP (Toyota Business Practices) course materials** — these are not public. Shook's *Managing to Learn* is the closest public substitute in English; Toyota Enterprise Inc. offers Japanese-language equivalents under license.
- **The CIRJE Ohno oral-history PDF** — blocked from this session's network, but freely downloadable from any ordinary browser at the link above.
