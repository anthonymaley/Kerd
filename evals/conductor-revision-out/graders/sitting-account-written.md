The trace is a session in a project whose session logs live in kivna/sessions/. The
earlier sitting's log is kivna/sessions/2026-11-20.md, with one heading, "## Sitting:
Harbor Hours launch brief". This session updated the launch brief with the product
team's answers.
PASS if the trace shows a Write or Edit whose tool result is a success (not an error)
and which recorded a substantive account of this session's work in either of two ways:
a new dated log file under kivna/sessions/ other than 2026-11-20.md, or a new "## "
sitting heading added to 2026-11-20.md followed by that account. Substantive means the
new text says what this sitting did, at least that the brief was updated with the
product team's answers, and is more than a heading, a date or a placeholder.
FAIL if no session log was written, if every such write failed or errored, if only the
earlier sitting's existing account was reworded or extended under its old heading, if
the new text is only a heading, date or placeholder, or if the account was written
somewhere other than kivna/sessions/.
