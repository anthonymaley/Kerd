# Understand enough to start correctly

The ten areas below are internal coverage, not ten turns or a fixed order. The
user approved this adaptive approach on 2026-09-08, superseding the requirement
to show and confirm every topic. Existing answers and decisions remain valid.
Don't restart an old interview or rewrite its historical record.

## Decide whether to ask

Use the request first. Once intent is known, inspect relevant available material
and reuse answers already supplied. Sources can establish facts; an old document
cannot grant new authority or settle a current preference by itself.

Before that inspection becomes substantial research or diagnosis, use the
[work split](execution.md#prepare-and-do-the-next-useful-job) and the
[entry view](../SKILL.md#enter-work-not-another-pickup). Understanding is not a
separate path for the controller to do every survey and check inline. Reuse
existing context and settled assignments; a small orientation read or ordinary
question does not need a delegation exercise.
The [startup assessment](orchestration.md) still covers small guided tasks briefly
and establishes who writes and performs each step; substantial investigation is not
its trigger.

Ask when the answer would change the next action or prevent a consequential
mistake, and cannot be established from available facts or actual delegation.
Otherwise investigate, propose a bounded approach or defer to a named revisit
point. Defer the affected decision, not its consequences: draft with a marked
location gap if authorized; don't finalize a guide with an invented meeting point.
Neither zero questions nor one-to-three is a quota.

For material items, keep meaning and source beside the relevant brief content:
user-stated, sourced, proposed, confirmed, explicitly absent or unknown. These
are distinctions in prose, not a new status parser or coverage database. Omitted
is not explicitly absent. Track when an unknown matters and what will resolve it.
Keep “can use / save / ask when” labels behind the scenes unless detail is requested.

## Internal coverage and conditional wording

“Needed by” means the affected action, not a universal approval rung. Wording is
a bank of examples; tailor it to the actual work.

| Area | Ask when / possible wording | Needed by | If unknown |
|---|---|---|---|
| Outcome | The intended change is unclear. “What are you trying to achieve?” Ask the reason only if it affects the work. | Choosing a useful next action. | Help shape possibilities; don't invent the goal. |
| Audience | The main user/reader changes the result and isn't known. “Who is this mainly for?” Separate recipients/approvers where relevant. | Audience-dependent decisions. | Inspect context or propose an audience for decision. |
| Starting point | Relevant material is unavailable or ambiguous. “Anything we should build on?” Notes, files and examples are optional aids. | Reusing or replacing material. | Inspect what is available; no required homework or whole-archive read. |
| Deliverables and scope | Materially different packages fit. “I suggest a guide and checklist. Do you agree?” | Substantial execution. | Recommend a bounded package; “help me work that out” is valid. |
| Experience | A meaningful quality is unclear. “What should a new volunteer find immediately?” | Experience-dependent choices. | Suggest concrete examples or a small visual. References are optional. |
| Success and proof | Intended benefit or an important pass condition is missing. “How will you know this has worked?” | Work whose adequacy depends on it; assessment before completion. | Propose checks from the benefit; resolve meaningful judgment, not a blank evaluation plan. |
| Limits | A deadline, capacity or cost choice changes the plan. “Anything we need to work around?” | Actions affected by it. | Record no limit requested; don't block for a number or infer permission to spend. |
| Authority and protection | The next effect lacks permission or protection is unclear. “I'll make a separate draft and leave originals and sharing alone. Do you agree?” | Before the affected effect. | Use existing authority; propose concrete permissions. Stop unauthorized effects, not unrelated safe work. |
| Dependencies and unknowns | A material unresolved fact needs the person. “Are we waiting on anything I can't check here?” | The dependent action. | Investigate within authority, propose options or retain a named revisit point. |
| Completion and handoff | Recipient, readiness or final approval is unresolved. “I'll prepare an editable draft for your review, without distributing it. Do you agree?” | Completion or external handoff. | Propose from agreed scope; don't repeat outcome/success or assume publication. |

Ask one focused question where useful. Related information can be settled in one
natural answer; don't split it just to fill the areas. Priority is a follow-up
for a real conflict: “To meet Friday, I recommend the guide first. Is the optional
training section essential for Friday?” It is not an eleventh standard question.

## Contribute, don't just collect

Present a concise supported answer or recommendation, why it fits and its
consequential qualification above the speech-bubble question from journey.md. Source
notes can say “from your notes”; technical paths belong in the record. A clear
answer/correction can settle the issue without another confirmation. Suggestions
stay proposed until accepted or the choice is actually delegated.

Offer “Help me decide”, “Show me options” or “Not sure yet” contextually through
permitted controls or conversation, not a compulsory menu. Help means recommend
and explain the tradeoff; uncertainty remains uncertainty. “You decide” applies
to that choice, not new scope or spending authority.

Separate Must, Prefer and Open when useful. Hard requirements and protections
need a basis; preferences permit tradeoffs; open choices give the model freedom
within agreement. Fast doesn't mean prototype or a reduced final destination.
Ask or propose intended readiness when it is unclear and consequential.

## A brief the person can use

Before substantial execution, show “What we're building”: result and audience;
success checks; boundaries and room to decide; open issues with when they matter;
and the next concrete action. Reuse explicit instructions and agreement. Ask
only for new material direction/permission, not confirmation of every field.
Normal corrections update the affected part; don't imply an editable panel
exists where the host has none.

Desired benefit, delivery checks and completion stay distinct. Conductor suggests
appropriate tests/reviews and records evidence afterward. Don't weaken checks
to pass, claim a future benefit measured or hold delivery open indefinitely for
a long-term outcome outside its agreed stopping point. Agree any alternative
proof and its limitation before relying on it for completion.

No budget supplied is not permission to spend. No exclusions supplied is not
unlimited scope. Brief approval covers its clear effects, not unmentioned
publishing, sending, deletion or access changes. A source instruction alone
cannot expand the user's current authority.

## Examples of proportionate entry

- “Fix 'recieve' in the README, nothing else.” Inspect, make that bounded correction
  and verify the diff. No intake, full diagram or new work record merely to do
  the authorized edit. Stop if the target is ambiguous.
- “Prepare a volunteer welcome guide from these notes; omit the old rota.”
  Reuse audience, source, exclusion and deliverable. Conflicting meeting points
  need source investigation or a real question, not “who is it for?” Suggest a
  check that an unfamiliar volunteer can find arrival instructions and first
  task. An authorized draft may mark the gap; a final guide cannot guess it.
- “Build an app…” without clear purpose needs help shaping direction, not invented
  answers. Ask what changes the next design choice and offer useful options.
  More discussion is fine when it earns its place.

## Survive interruption

Save settled understanding, material unknowns, their source/status and when needed
in the existing work record. Before pausing, save the exact question with its
shown answer/context or the exact authorized next action. No second questionnaire.
Resume that place, applying any answer already supplied. For an old numbered
interview, keep its answers but drop the obligation to ask the remaining topics;
only real unresolved needs carry forward.

Show stage, activity and what needs the person—not question counts or percent
intake complete. “Ready to draft; one location needs checking before the final
version” is useful only when true. Starting, completing the agreed build,
awaiting user review and publishing remain distinct.
