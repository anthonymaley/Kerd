- **THE FUNNEL DRIVER IS AN UMBRELLA ABOVE CONDUCTOR, NOT A GRADUATION INTO IT — Tony, 2026-08-23, and it dissolves `funnel-driver`'s killer risk rather than managing it.** His proposal, verbatim: *"if its working as is today (maybe not ideal though, but working) maybe we should have this as a separate branch/command or an overriding one (umbrella) for what we have today… that forces the stages and uses conductor and the other tools withing the skill, without breaking conductor basic as it is now, maybe a conductor le repo is enough for just getting things done without all the process ad you have no intention of getting past mvp etc so why use the funnel approach. then we can keep building one bit at a time under that umbrella, while keeping what is there useful still"*. **Why this is a different answer and not a rephrasing:** `docs/product/funnel-driver.md` assumed throughout that the driver arrives by *graduating functions into conductor* — its first killer risk is worded *"Editing conductor breaks the only working instance of half the system's functions"*, and its countermeasure is a discipline (one graduation at a time, guarded substitutions, never removals) that must be obeyed on every future edit forever. **An umbrella that calls conductor without changing it retires that risk by construction instead** — there is no edit to conductor, so there is nothing to break, and the discipline stops being load-bearing. It also satisfies the *second*, opposing killer risk (*"a driver that reads the funnel but obliges nothing"* — twice measured on this repo, complete CI-enforced machinery producing zero artifacts) because the umbrella **forces** the stages rather than advising them. Both killers answered at once, which is what the frame said made the slicing hard. **And it is consistent with his own 2026-08-07 shape rule, not a departure from it** — *"not a skill per stage no — one skill for all"*: the umbrella is one skill covering every stage, with conductor left underneath as the session-discipline tool it already is. **Proportionality becomes the door you walk through, not a setting.** A repo that will never pass MVP keeps plain conductor and pays no process cost; a product that needs the funnel enters through the umbrella. That is cheaper than any config-driven sizing and it is the concrete answer to his *"without it becomeing a 100year project"* constraint. **The constraint that makes it actually safe rather than nominally safe, recorded as the rule to build under: the umbrella may CALL conductor but must never REQUIRE conductor to change.** The moment a slice needs conductor to behave differently, the killer risk is back in full and the slice must either do the work itself or stop. **Consequence for the board:** this does not create a new item — it rewrites `funnel-driver`'s mechanism, killer risk and slice plan, and unparks the oldest stalled item on the board (framed 2026-08-07, at design, untouched across two sittings). **Naming, under his own currency rule of the same morning:** he offered `/concert`, `/project` and `/product`; `concert` extends the internal producer/composer/conductor metaphor and is exactly the invented name that rule forbids, and the thing being walked loops after launch rather than ending, which is product-shaped rather than project-shaped. Not settled here — his call, deliberately not over-baked.
